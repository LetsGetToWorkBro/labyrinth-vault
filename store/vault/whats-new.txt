First public build.

Both chains sign: Bitcoin PSBTs and Monero unsigned sets are read, rendered in full, and signed on this device. A built-in demo transaction walks the whole flow with no companion device, against demo keys that can never touch a real coin.

The lifecycle is real end to end. Keys are generated on device from the platform's CSPRNG, sealed under your passphrase with Argon2id and XChaCha20-Poly1305, stored passcode-bound in the keychain, unlocked on launch, and wiped the moment the app leaves the foreground.
