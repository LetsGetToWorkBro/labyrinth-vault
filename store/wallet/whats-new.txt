First TestFlight build.

The complete interface: send, receive, activity, the vault pairing screen, the swap flow, and the state that matters most, which is a returned transaction that does not match the one that was approved.

Accounts, and two kinds of them. An account paired from a vault is watch-only on this phone forever and signs across the airgap. An account made or restored here keeps its recovery phrase in the keychain and signs on this phone behind Face ID, per signature, for the smaller balances a phone is the right place for. Every screen that can act on an account says which kind it is.

Both chains read real data through a node you choose; there is no default node, because picking one would be picking who watches your addresses. Bitcoin discovers, lists coins and history, and broadcasts through any Esplora server. Monero scans on the device with your view key and proves every found amount against the chain. Spends are subtracted once the key images are known: an account on this phone computes its own, and a vault-paired account gets them from a QR round trip with the vault. Dollar figures come only from Labyrinth's relay, which serves every client one cached answer so no price service ever sees you. Otherwise balances are shown in coin. A wallet watching nothing says so instead of showing a fixture.
